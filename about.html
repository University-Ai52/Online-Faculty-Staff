Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees of BSNU University for Science and Technology plays a vital role in shaping the strategic direction of the university. The Board is responsible for ensuring academic excellence, institutional integrity, and sustainable growth.

Through effective governance and oversight, the Board supports innovation, transparency, and the continuous development of educational programs that meet national and international standards.

President

The President of BSNU University leads the institution with a clear vision focused on academic quality, research advancement, and community engagement. The President works closely with faculty members and administrative leaders to create an inspiring learning environment.

Under the President’s leadership, BSNU continues to strengthen its position as a leading university for science and technology in Egypt and the region.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality online resources and support for faculty and staff to enhance their teaching and professional development.

Our Vision

We envision a collaborative online community where faculty and staff can share knowledge, access resources, and grow professionally.

Our Values

  • Collaboration: We believe in the power of working together to achieve common goals.
  • Innovation: We are committed to embracing new technologies and methods to improve our services.
  • Excellence: We strive for excellence in everything we do, from resource creation to user support.

Values & Principles

  • Academic Excellence: Commitment to high standards in teaching and learning.
  • Integrity: Upholding ethical values, transparency, and accountability.
  • Innovation: Encouraging creativity, research, and technological advancement.
  • Respect & Diversity: Promoting inclusiveness and respect for cultural differences.
  • Community Engagement: Serving society through education, research, and partnerships.
