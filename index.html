Welcome To BSNU

BSNU University for Science and Technology A Leading University in Egypt.

Welcome to BSNU University for Science and Technology (BSNU), the home away from home to more than 35,000 students from all over the globe.

The Community fosters a sense of pride in your own identity while encouraging you to explore the richness of diverse cultures. As well as the support and guidance you need for your academic and personal goals. 

University History

Beni-Suef University, established in 1976, has a rich history of academic excellence and community service. Over the decades, it has grown to become a leading institution in Egypt, offering a wide range of programs and fostering research and innovation.

The university has played a pivotal role in the development of the Beni-Suef region, contributing to social and economic progress through education and outreach initiatives. With a commitment to quality education and research, Beni-Suef University continues to shape the future of its students and the broader community.
